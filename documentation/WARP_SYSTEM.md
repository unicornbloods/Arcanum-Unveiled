# Warp System

Warp represents the mental strain and corruption caused by delving into forbidden knowledge or handling dangerous eldritch objects.

## Warp Types

1.  **Permanent Warp**: Gained by completing certain "Forbidden" research. It can never be removed.
2.  **Sticky Warp**: Gained by crafting eldritch items or performing dark rituals. It can be reduced using **Sanity Soap**.
3.  **Temporary Warp**: Gained by using certain items or being attacked by eldritch creatures. It decays over time and is easily removed with **Sanity Soap**.

## Warp Events

As your total Warp level increases, you may experience "events":
- **Level 1+**: Strange whispers and unsettling messages.
- **Level 10+**: Minor hallucinations, like seeing **Mind Spiders** that vanish when approached.
- **Level 25+**: Physical manifestations, such as hunger, blurred vision, or being attacked by **Eldritch Guardians**.
- **High Warp**: Significant hazards like **Death Gaze** or intense hallucinations.

## Management

- **Sanity Checker**: A tool to view your current Warp levels.
- **Sanity Soap**: A consumable item that reduces Sticky and Temporary Warp. Its effectiveness is increased if used while standing in **Purifying Fluid** or while under the effect of **Warp Ward**.
- **Warp Ward**: A potion effect that protects against Warp gain and increases the effectiveness of Sanity Soap.

## Commands (Admin)

- `/tc warp <player> query`: Check a player's warp levels.
- `/tc warp <player> add <perm|sticky|temp> <amount>`: Add warp to a player.
- `/tc warp <player> set <perm|sticky|temp> <amount>`: Set a player's warp level.
