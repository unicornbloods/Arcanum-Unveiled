# The Eldritch Domain (The Outer Lands)

The Eldritch Domain is a dimension of madness and ancient architecture, home to the Eldritch Guardians and the source of the deepest magical secrets.

## Accessing the Domain
To enter the Outer Lands, a player must usually find an **Eldritch Obelisk** in the overworld. By activating the obelisk with an **Eldritch Eye**, a portal to the domain is opened.

## The Maze
The Outer Lands is a vast, procedurally generated maze created using the **Jigsaw System**.
- **Corridors**: Long, winding paths filled with traps and spawners.
- **Special Rooms**: Unique chambers containing rare loot, Aura Nodes, or ancient machinery.
- **Boss Rooms**: Large arenas where powerful Eldritch entities reside.

## Eldritch Mechanics
- **Unbreaking Blocks**: Many blocks in the Eldritch Domain are warded and cannot be broken by conventional means until the "Lock" of the area is opened.
- **Sanity Loss**: Spending time in the Outer Lands increases a player's **Warp** level significantly.
- **Eldritch Spawners**: These unique spawners produce Eldritch Guardians and Mind Spiders at a rapid rate.

## Eldritch Blocks
- **Eldritch Stone**: The primary building material of the domain.
- **Eldritch Altar**: A mysterious pedestal used in ancient rituals.
- **Ancient Lock**: A block that prevents passage until a specific condition is met or a key is used.
