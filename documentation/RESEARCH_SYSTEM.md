# Research System

The Research System is the heart of Arcanum Unveiled, allowing players to discover new aspects, study forbidden knowledge, and unlock powerful magic.

## Getting Started

1.  **Thaumonomicon**: Craft a Thaumonomicon to track your progress.
2.  **Thaumometer**: Use a Thaumometer to scan blocks, items, and entities. Scanning reveals the aspects contained within and grants you initial research knowledge.
3.  **Research Table**: Use Scribe's Tools and Paper at a Research Table to combine aspects and complete research notes.

## Administrative Commands

For testing and server management, several commands are available:

- `/tc research <player> query`: List all completed research for a player.
- `/tc research <player> give <research_key>`: Unlocks a specific research node for the player.
- `/tc research <player> give all`: Unlocks all available research for the player.
- `/tc research <player> take <research_key>`: (Coming soon) Removes research progress.

## Categories

- **Basics**: Foundational knowledge of Thaumaturgy.
- **Thaumaturgy**: Focusing on wands, caps, rods, and foci.
- **Alchemy**: The art of dissolving items into essentia and crafting via the crucible.
- **Artifice**: Advanced magical machinery and automation.
- **Golemancy**: Creating and commanding magical constructs.
- **Eldritch**: Forbidden knowledge of the Outer Lands.
