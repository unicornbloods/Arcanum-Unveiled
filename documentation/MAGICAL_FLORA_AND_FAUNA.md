# Magical Flora and Fauna

The world of Arcanum Unveiled is filled with unique plants and creatures infused with magic.

## Trees

- **Greatwood**: Massive trees with dark wood. Their logs are dense and can hold more magical energy
  than standard wood.
- **Silverwood**: Rare, shimmering trees with white wood. Their logs can sometimes contain Aura
  Nodes, and their wood is essential for high-tier magical items.

## Plants

- **Cinderpearl**: Found in deserts. These glowing flowers produce Blaze Powder when harvested.
- **Shimmerleaf**: Found under Silverwood trees. They are a primary source of Quicksilver.
- **Vishroom**: Glowing mushrooms found in magical biomes. They are highly hallucinogenic and
  contain pure magic.
- **Mana Pods**: Grow on the underside of Greatwood logs. They produce Mana Beans, which contain
  random Aspects.

## Creatures

- **Wisp**: Ethereal beings made of pure energy. They drop Wisp Essence and are often found near
  Aura Nodes.
- **Pech**: Small, goblin-like creatures found in forests. They love to trade for gold and other
  precious items.
- **Brainy Zombie**: A more intelligent variant of the standard zombie. They drop Zombie Brains,
  which are used in advanced Golemancy.
- **Fire Bat**: Small, aggressive bats found in the Nether. They explode on contact.
- **Eldritch Guardian**: Ancient protectors of the Eldritch Domain. They appear to players who have
  high levels of Warp.
- **Crimson Cultists**: A mysterious group of humans who worship the Outer Gods. They can be found
  in small camps around the world.
